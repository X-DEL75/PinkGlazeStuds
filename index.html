The Brand

PinkGlaze

PinkGlaze was built on a simple belief: every client deserves to feel polished, confident and camera-ready. From soft everyday glam to full bridal transformation and professional photoshoot looks, we approach every face with precision and luxury.

Our studio is located at 6, Abraham Afolabi Street, off General, Abule Egba, Lagos. We work strictly within Lagos — no out-of-state travel — so we can give every appointment the focused attention it deserves.

Services include professional makeup (Owambe, casual, bridal, photoshoot), hair installation, microblading, and studio photography.

Questions

Frequently Asked

Where is PinkGlaze based?

We are studio-based at 6, Abraham Afolabi Street, off General, Abule Egba, Lagos, Nigeria.

Do you travel outside Lagos?

No. We currently do not take bookings outside Lagos. All services are within Lagos only so we can maintain quality and punctuality.

What services do you offer?

Professional makeup (Owambe, casual, bridal, photoshoot), hair installation, microblading, and studio photography.

How do I book an appointment?

Reach out via WhatsApp, Instagram or email. We recommend booking in advance, especially for bridal and photoshoot dates.

Is the bridal price inclusive?

The ₦50,000 bridal rate is for services within Lagos. Additional travel or overnight arrangements are not included as we stay Lagos-based.

Do you offer home service?

We primarily work from the studio for best lighting and equipment control. Limited in-Lagos home service may be discussed on a case-by-case basis.
